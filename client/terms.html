Terms of Service

Orders Are Subject To Confirmation

Submitting an order through this website does not create a binding sale on its own. Our team reviews every order for product availability, current price, and delivery feasibility, then contacts you to confirm. An order is only confirmed once we have spoken with you and agreed the details.

Prices And Availability

Live animal prices and availability change. Where a price is not confirmed on the website, we will provide the current price before you commit. Delivery fees are estimates until confirmed. We may decline or adjust an order if a product becomes unavailable or if the listed information is incorrect.

Payment

No payment is taken automatically through this website. Payment arrangements are agreed directly with our team after your order is confirmed. Please do not send money to anyone claiming to represent us without first confirming our official contact details on this site.

Delivery And Collection

Delivery dates and time windows are agreed during confirmation and may be affected by weather, transport, and the welfare of the animals. Someone must be available to receive live animals at the agreed time. See our delivery information for details.

Live Animal Welfare

We take reasonable care to supply healthy animals and to transport them safely. Once animals are handed over, their care becomes the responsibility of the customer. Please inspect your order on receipt and raise any concern with us immediately.

Website Content

We aim to keep product descriptions accurate, but photographs and weights are indicative. Product images may be representative rather than an exact individual animal. We may update the website, products, and these terms as our business develops.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through our contact page.
