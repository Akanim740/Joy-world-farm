Returns & Cancellations

Cancelling Or Changing An Order

You can ask to cancel or change an order before it is confirmed or prepared. Please contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp or by phone. Once an order has been prepared, dispatched, or collected, changes may not be possible.

Live Animals

Live animals cannot be returned once they have left our care, because their condition after handover cannot be controlled. Please inspect your animals carefully at the point of delivery or collection and tell us immediately if something is wrong.

Concerns On Delivery

If an order arrives damaged, incomplete, or not as agreed, contact us the same day with your order reference and, where possible, a photo. We will investigate and, where the issue is our responsibility, agree a fair solution such as replacement, credit, or refund.

Non-Returnable Items

Perishable and live products are not eligible for a change-of-mind return. Other items may be considered for return if they are unused and in their original condition, at our discretion.

Refunds

Any agreed refund is made using the same payment method used for the order, unless we agree otherwise. Timing depends on the payment method and is confirmed with you directly.
