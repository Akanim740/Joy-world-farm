Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver within Port Harcourt?

Yes, delivery and collection arrangements can be made. Delivery fees depend on your location and are confirmed with your order.

Are the prices on the website final?

Some listed products may show demonstration pricing. Please contact us on WhatsApp or by phone to confirm current prices and availability.

Do I have to pay online?

No. You place an order through the website, then we contact you to confirm the order and arrange payment directly.

Do you sell to farms and retailers in bulk?

Yes. Please use the wholesale enquiry form and we will get back to you about bulk pricing.

Can I order on WhatsApp?

Yes. Every product and the checkout page have WhatsApp order buttons that open a prefilled message for you.
