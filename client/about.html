About Joy World Farm

Who We Are

Joy World Farm is a developing agricultural business based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We supply a variety of poultry products — including chicks and turkeys at different growth stages — as well as catfish to farmers, households, and businesses in the region.

What We Offer

Our product catalogue includes day-old chicks, three-week-old chicks, grow-out birds, day-old turkeys, turkey grow-outs, and catfish products. We work with multiple breeds including broilers, layers, and noilers, and continue to expand our offerings.

Our Location

We are proudly based in Port Harcourt and serve customers across Rivers State. Collection from the farm and local delivery arrangements can be discussed when you place your order.

Get In Touch

We are growing and refining our business every day. If you have questions about our products, availability, or wholesale options, please reach out through our contact page or send us a message on WhatsApp.
