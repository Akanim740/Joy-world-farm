Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, landmark, and any order notes. If you place an order for someone else, we also collect the recipient's name and phone number.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and confirm orders, arrange delivery or collection, answer enquiries, and keep records of transactions. We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp, or email about your order.

Who Can See Your Information

Order and enquiry details are visible only to authorised Joy World Farm staff. We do not publish customer names, phone numbers, or addresses on the public website. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing With Delivery Staff

To complete a delivery, we may share the recipient name, phone number, address, landmark, and delivery instructions with the person delivering your order. We share only what is needed to complete the delivery.

Data Retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to run our business, meet accounting needs, and resolve disputes. If you would like us to correct or delete your information, contact us and we will review your request.

Cookies And Local Storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart and, where applicable, your order tracking token. We do not use advertising cookies. Your session cookie is used for the admin area only.

Contact

For privacy questions, please reach us through the contact page or by phone or WhatsApp using the details shown there.

This policy describes our current practices and may be updated as our business grows.
