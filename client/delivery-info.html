Delivery Information

Where We Deliver

We currently arrange delivery within Port Harcourt and surrounding areas of Rivers State. Delivery availability and fees depend on your exact location. The zones shown at checkout are estimates and are confirmed by our team before payment.

Delivery Fees

Delivery fees are calculated by zone and displayed at checkout before you place your order. If your area is not listed, please contact us on WhatsApp or by phone so we can confirm whether delivery is possible and what it will cost.

Timeframes

We aim to confirm every order as quickly as possible. Delivery timing depends on product availability, preparation requirements, weather, and your location. Live animals are only dispatched when it is safe to transport them. We will agree a delivery date and time window with you when we confirm your order.

Live Animal Handling

Chicks, poults, and grow-out birds are transported in ventilated crates. We ask that someone is available to receive the animals promptly, especially in hot weather. If you cannot receive a delivery, please tell us as early as possible so we can reschedule.

Collection From The Farm

You may choose collection from the farm instead of delivery. Collection has no delivery fee. We will confirm the collection address, date, and time with you after your order is placed.
